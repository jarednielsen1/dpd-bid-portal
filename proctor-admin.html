Process Bid
Run after bid cycle closes — splits names and assigns supervisors
What this does
  • Splits every employee's name into FirstName and LastName fields
  • For each PO and SC who placed a bid, finds their sector and assigns the SGT who bid that same sector
  • Writes SupervisorFirstName, SupervisorLastName, and SupervisorBadge to BidRoster
  • If no SGT bid a sector, that field is left blank — processing continues for all other employees
